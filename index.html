About Me

Building healthier
communities together

I am a third-year Health Promotion undergraduate at Rajarata University of Sri Lanka, passionate about working with communities to create sustainable improvements in health and well-being.

My interests focus on facilitating community empowerment and ownership, strengthening community capacity to identify and address health concerns, and promoting participatory approaches to health development. I am committed to supporting community-led action and fostering environments that enable individuals and communities to gain greater control over the factors that influence their health.

Through my field practical in Palugeswewa, I have applied these principles alongside mothers' groups, elders' groups, and children's groups. Beyond academics, I am an active member of the Health Promotion Society and the Gavel Club, continuing to develop my skills in public speaking, group facilitation, and health advocacy.
